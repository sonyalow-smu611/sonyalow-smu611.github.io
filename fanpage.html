Everything you need to know about Mr worldwide handsome

Kim Seok-jin, known professionally as Jin, is a South Korean singer, songwriter, and vocalist of the global boy band BTS. Debuting in 2013, he is celebrated for his emotive tenor vocals, solo tracks like "Epiphany", and his role as the oldest member of the group.
